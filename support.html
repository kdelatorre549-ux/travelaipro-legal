What Travel AI Pro does

Travel AI Pro prepares you for a destination before you arrive. Each guide covers the apps locals actually use and how to set them up, how to pay for things, how the transport works and what the signage looks like, plus walking routes for each region worth visiting.

Everything is stored on your phone. The guides work in airplane mode, on landing with no data plan, and underground on a metro — which is when you need them most.

Do I need an account?

No. Every guide works without signing in. An account only syncs your saved trips and lets you restore a subscription on a new phone.

Why is a country locked?

Taiwan is free forever. Every other guide, and AI-planned days, are part of the subscription.

Guide depth is labelled honestly in the app. A guide marked "growing" has fewer visual walkthroughs than one marked "flagship" — check the label before you subscribe for a specific country.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Travel AI Pro, then Cancel Subscription.

After cancelling you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we never see your payment details and cannot issue one. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find Travel AI Pro and choose Request a Refund.

I paid but the app still shows a paywall

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. That asks Apple directly what you own and usually fixes it.

If it does not, make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account you bought with, then email us.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, then Delete account. That permanently removes your profile and saved trips. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a subscription — cancel that separately in iOS Settings.

Something is wrong or out of date

Prices, fares and app links change. If you find something wrong, tell us which country and which screen and we will correct it in the next update.

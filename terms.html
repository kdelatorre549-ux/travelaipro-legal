What this app is

Travel AI Pro is a travel preparation guide. It tells you which apps to install, how to pay for things, how to move around, and what to see in the countries it covers.

It is a guide, not a booking service. We do not sell tickets, take payments for travel, or act as an agent for anyone.

Accuracy, and its limits

Guide content is researched and re-verified periodically, and every app store link is checked automatically. Even so, prices, opening hours, routes, fares and visa rules change constantly and without notice.

Treat everything here as a well-informed starting point and confirm anything that matters — visas, entry requirements, medical care, last trains — with the official source before you rely on it.

Where the AI concierge is offered, its answers are generated and can be wrong. Do not rely on them for legal, medical, financial or immigration decisions.

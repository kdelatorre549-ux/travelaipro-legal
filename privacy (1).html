Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2027

Version: 1.0.0

Effective Date: January 1, 2027

1. Introduction

Travel AI Pro ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping addresses, payment information
  • Content You Create: Travel itineraries, destination preferences, notes, user-generated guides, photos, and travel logs
  • Communication Data: Messages through our platform, customer support inquiries, feedback, and survey responses

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, time spent, links clicked, features used, searches performed, referral sources
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates (with your permission), IP address-derived location, destination history
  • Cookies and Tracking: Session cookies for authentication, analytics cookies, preference cookies for customization

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from social media platforms, travel partners, booking integrations, and analytics providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Travel AI Pro uses collected information for:

  • Service Delivery: Creating accounts, providing recommendations, processing bookings
  • Personalization: Customizing your experience based on preferences
  • Communication: Sending updates, customer support, promotional content (with consent)
  • Analytics: Understanding usage patterns to improve the platform
  • Legal Compliance: Maintaining records, detecting fraud, enforcing agreements
  • Marketing: With your consent, sending newsletters and promotional offers

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, email services, cloud storage providers
  • Business Partners: Travel companies, hotels, tour operators (only with your consent)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect safety
  • Successors: In case of merger, acquisition, or bankruptcy

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for:

  • Active Accounts: Duration of account existence plus 30 days after deletion
  • Deleted Accounts: Backup data retained for 90 days
  • Legal Holds: As required by applicable laws
  • Analytics: Aggregated, non-identifiable data retained indefinitely

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Portability

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data in a machine-readable format
  • Request a copy of all information we hold about you
  • Port your data to another service

6.2 Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data. We will comply within 30 days, except where data must be retained for legal purposes.

6.3 Opt-Out

  • Marketing Communications: Unsubscribe via email footer or account settings
  • Cookies: Adjust browser settings or use our cookie preference tool
  • Location Tracking: Disable GPS permissions in your device settings
  • Analytics: Opt out via Do Not Track signals

6.4 Correction

You can update or correct your account information anytime in your profile settings.

7. Data Security

Travel AI Pro implements industry-standard safeguards:

  • Encryption: End-to-end encryption for sensitive data in transit
  • Storage: Encrypted databases with access controls
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication for account protection
  • Monitoring: Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Staff Training: Employee education on data protection

However, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will promptly delete it.

9. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

European Union (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • We process data based on consent, contract, legal obligation, or legitimate interests
  • Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@travelaipro.com
  • Right to lodge complaint with your local data protection authority

California (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, and shared
  • Delete personal information collected
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@travelaipro.com.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred, stored, and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your home country. By using Travel AI Pro, you consent to such transfers, subject to appropriate safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

Travel AI Pro may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or by posting a notice on our website. Your continued use of the Service after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
